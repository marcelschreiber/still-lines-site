Privacy policy

Still Lines. Last updated: 11 October 2026

Still Lines does not collect any data about you.

  • There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send anything to me or to any third party. It has no server.
  • Your progress (solved levels, streak, settings) is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for the app, the same results are also stored in your own iCloud account so they can sync between your devices. That data belongs to you and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; I cannot see it.
  • Apple may give developers anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (for example downloads) from people who chose to share them. They contain nothing that identifies you.

If you write to puzzle-apps@proton.me, I use your email address only to answer you and do not share it.

If this ever changes, I will update this page before the update that changes it is released.

Contact: puzzle-apps@proton.me
