Still Lines

A calm connect-the-dots puzzle.

Questions, bug reports or ideas: puzzle-apps@proton.me

Please mention your iPhone model, the iOS version and, for a bug, the level or daily number. I read every message and answer when I can.

Is the game free?

Yes. Every level, every daily and all stats are included. There are no ads and no purchases.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. It works offline.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

If iCloud is on for Still Lines (Settings, your name, iCloud, Apps Using iCloud), your results sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Results synced through iCloud can be removed under Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Still Lines.
